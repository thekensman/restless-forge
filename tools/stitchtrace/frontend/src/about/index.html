About StitchTrace

Free online tool to convert images into embroidery machine files (.PES, .DST, .JEF).

What It Does

StitchTrace was built as a free, browser-based alternative to expensive tools. Free online tool to convert images into embroidery machine files (.PES, .DST, .JEF).

About Your Data

Privacy: Nothing you enter is sent to a server. All processing happens in your browser.
