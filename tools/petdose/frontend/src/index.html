⚠ Before you use PetDose

PetDose shows general reference ranges compiled from published veterinary sources. It is not veterinary advice. Safe dosing depends on your pet's breed, age, weight, health conditions, pregnancy status, and other medications — never give a medication, change a dose, or use a prescription drug without your veterinarian's direction.

If your pet swallowed something toxic or is in distress, contact your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435 now.
