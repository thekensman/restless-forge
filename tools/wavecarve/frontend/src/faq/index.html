Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about WaveCarve.

Is WaveCarve free?

Completely free. No account, no subscription, no hidden fees.

Does WaveCarve upload my files?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your files never leave your device. WaveCarve has no backend server.

What browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. Works on desktop and mobile.

Is it open source?

Yes. Code is on GitHub.
