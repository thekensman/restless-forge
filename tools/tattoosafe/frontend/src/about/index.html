About TattooSafe

Free AR tattoo preview & pricing calculator — everything runs in your browser.

What It Does

TattooSafe lets you see how a tattoo design will look on your body using your phone or laptop camera. Upload any image, activate the camera, and drag your design into position. The pricing calculator estimates cost based on size, style, placement, and artist experience level using 2024–2025 US market data.

How It Works

The AR preview uses the browser Camera API (getUserMedia) and HTML5 Canvas for real-time compositing at 60fps. The silhouette preview generates inline SVGs with anatomically proportioned body outlines scaled to your height. The pricing engine calculates session time from tattoo area × complexity, adjusted for placement difficulty and session overhead.

About Your Data

Privacy: Nothing you enter is sent to a server. Camera feed stays on your device. Images are never uploaded. All processing happens in your browser.
