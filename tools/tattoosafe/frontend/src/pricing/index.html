Tattoo Pricing Calculator

Estimate your tattoo cost based on dimensions, style, placement, and artist experience level.

How Pricing Works

Most artists charge by the hour. Size determines session time. TattooSafe estimates total session time from your tattoo's area, multiplied by a time-per-square-inch rate that varies with style complexity. Setup time (~30 min) and breaks are factored in.

What Affects Price

Size: The biggest factor. A 2-inch wrist piece: ~30 min. Full back piece: 30+ hours across multiple sessions.

Style: Simple outlines are fastest. Photorealistic portraits take 3–4× longer per square inch.

Placement: Ribs, hands, feet, neck take longer due to pain and difficult angles.

Artist: Apprentices $80–120/hr, experienced $120–200/hr, specialists $200–350/hr, celebrity $350–500+/hr.
