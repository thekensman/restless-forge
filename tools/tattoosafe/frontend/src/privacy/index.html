Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

Data Collection

TattooSafe does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account required.

Camera Access

The AR preview uses the browser Camera API. Video frames are processed locally and never transmitted. Camera stops when you click Stop or close the tab.

File Handling

Uploaded images stay in browser memory via the File API. Never sent to a server. Discarded on page close.

For the full privacy policy, see the Restless Forge Privacy Policy.
