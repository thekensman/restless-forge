Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the AR preview, pricing calculator, and privacy.

How does the AR preview work?

Upload your design, activate your camera, and the design is overlaid on the live video feed using Canvas. Drag to position, pinch to resize, use the rotation slider to angle it. No body tracking AI — you position manually for precise control.

Does TattooSafe store my photos?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Camera feed is never recorded or transmitted. Uploaded images stay in browser memory and are discarded when you close the tab. TattooSafe has no backend server.

How accurate is the pricing calculator?

It provides estimates based on aggregated US market data from 2024–2025 tattoo industry surveys. Actual prices vary by region, shop, and artist. Use it as a starting point, then get a quote during your consultation.

What body parts are supported?

16 placements: inner/outer forearm, upper arm, shoulder, wrist, chest, upper back, ribs, sternum, lower back, thigh, calf, ankle, back of neck, behind ear, and back of hand.

Does the preview track my body / wrap around my arm automatically?

No — and we're upfront about that. TattooSafe uses no AI or body tracking, so the overlay is a static try-on aid: you line the design up against your reflection in the camera, then screenshot it. The optional "Curve around a limb" toggle bends the design around a cylinder whose curvature you set with the curvature slider — a manual approximation of how a design sits on a rounded surface, not automatic limb detection.

My design has a background — will it show as a box?

"Remove flat background" (on by default) flood-fills a solid, uniform backdrop to transparent, so logos, line art, and clean-cut designs land on skin without a dark box under the multiply blend. It intentionally leaves photographic backgrounds (busy scenes, gradient skies) alone — cleanly cutting those out needs AI segmentation, which this tool doesn't include. For best results, upload a design on a plain white or solid-colour background.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Works in any modern mobile browser. Touch gestures (drag, pinch-to-zoom) work for the AR preview.

Is TattooSafe free?

Completely free. No account, no subscription, no hidden fees. Open source.
