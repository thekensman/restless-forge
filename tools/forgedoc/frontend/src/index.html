ForgeDoc: Free browser-only PDF toolkit

Merge, split, rotate, watermark, and build PDFs from images — entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device: no uploads, no accounts, no task limits, no watermarks (except the ones you add on purpose).

Pick two or more PDFs. They merge in the order selected.

    Pick one PDF, then choose pages like 1-3, 7, 10-15.

    Pick JPG/PNG images. One page per image, in order.

      Pick one PDF, choose pages and an angle.

      Stamp text across every page.

      Everything runs locally via pdf-lib. Compression and PDF→image export are coming in v2.
