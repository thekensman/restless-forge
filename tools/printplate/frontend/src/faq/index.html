Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PrintPlate.

Is PrintPlate free?

Completely free. No account, no subscription, no hidden fees.

Does PrintPlate upload my files?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your files never leave your device. PrintPlate has no backend server.

What browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. Works on desktop and mobile.

Is it open source?

Yes. Code is on GitHub.
