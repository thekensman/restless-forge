Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about LensMatch.

Is LensMatch free?

Completely free. No account, no subscription, no hidden fees.

Does LensMatch upload my files?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your files never leave your device. LensMatch has no backend server.

What browsers are supported?

Any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. Works on desktop and mobile.

Is it open source?

Yes. Code is on GitHub.
