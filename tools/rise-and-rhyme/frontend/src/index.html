Rise & Rhyme

Wake up to a song about your day. Rise & Rhyme reads tomorrow's calendar, writes custom lyrics, and plays them over an upbeat backing track as your morning alarm.

☁ Cloud-assisted tool. Your calendar's public iCal URL is sent to the Restless Forge server once per evening to generate lyrics with Claude. Nothing else leaves your browser — see the privacy page. v1 needs this tab open overnight to fire the alarm.
