Why This Calculator Exists

The average American has 4.5 streaming subscriptions alone, plus gym memberships, cloud storage, productivity apps, and meal kits. Studies show 89% of us underestimate what we actually spend — guessing around $62/month when the real number is over $91. And that's just the average. Many people spend $200+ without realizing it.

The "hours of life energy" column is the real gut-punch. When you see that a streaming service you use once a month costs 8 hours of your working life per year, the cancel button starts looking very attractive.
