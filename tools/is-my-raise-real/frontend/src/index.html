Why This Calculator Exists

Most people hear "3% raise" and feel good about it. They shouldn't — not without checking what inflation did that year. A raise that doesn't beat inflation is a pay cut wearing a congratulations card. This tool does the math your employer hopes you won't.

The Salary Erosion tab is the one that hurts. It shows the cumulative gap between what you earn and what you'd need to have the same buying power as the day you were hired. For many people, that gap is tens of thousands of dollars.

CPI data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Built by Kenneth Cross. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
