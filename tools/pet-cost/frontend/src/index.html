Why This Calculator Exists

Roughly 20% of adopted pets are returned to shelters within six months. Financial stress is one of the most common reasons. That's heartbreaking for the animal and the family. This calculator exists to prevent that — not by discouraging pet ownership, but by making sure you go in with realistic numbers.

The costs people forget: dental cleanings ($200–700 per session), end-of-life care (euthanasia, cremation, or burial: $200–1,500), pet deposits on apartments ($200–500), and the big one — emergency vet visits that average $800–1,500 and can exceed $5,000 for surgery.
