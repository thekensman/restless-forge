Why This Calculator Exists

We all do it. We drive across town to save a few dollars on gas, clip coupons for an hour to save $8, or buy bulk toilet paper we have nowhere to store. It feels like saving. But when you factor in the value of your time — the time you could spend earning, resting, or just not being in a parking lot — many "savings" turn out to cost more than they save.

This isn't about never being frugal. It's about being frugal where it actually works. Some bulk buys are genuinely great deals. Some coupons are worth clipping. This tool helps you tell the difference.
