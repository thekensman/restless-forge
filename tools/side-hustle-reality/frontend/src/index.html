Why This Calculator Exists

Gig platforms show you gross earnings because the number looks good. They don't subtract gas, vehicle depreciation, self-employment tax (15.3% — you pay both halves of FICA), phone costs, or the dead time between rides. When you do the math, many gig workers earn less than minimum wage. That doesn't mean all gigs are bad — some are genuinely profitable, especially with the right vehicle and market. This tool helps you see the real number.

Vehicle depreciation uses the 2026 IRS standard mileage rate of $0.725/mile as a proxy for total vehicle costs. Built by Kenneth Cross. Everything runs in your browser.
