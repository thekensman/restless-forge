Sunk cost fallacy detected. You've already spent more on repairs than the item is worth. Past repairs can't be recovered — they shouldn't influence whether the next repair makes sense. The question isn't "I've already invested so much" — it's "does this next repair, on its own merits, make sense compared to buying new?"

Why This Calculator Exists

Most repair-vs-replace tools online are built by companies that profit from one answer. Repair shops want you to repair. Retailers want you to replace. Consumer Reports has a version, but it's behind a paywall. This calculator has no agenda, no affiliate links to appliance stores, and no "schedule a repair" button. Just the math.

The Sunk Cost Detector on Tab 2 addresses the most common mistake: "I've already put $800 into this thing, I can't give up now." Past spending is gone. It shouldn't influence the next decision. This tool helps you see that clearly.
