CHANGED IN 25
  - fonts: the four faces the page uses are served from assets/fonts under
    the SIL Open Font License, so the page makes no request outside its own
    folder. Build 24 loaded them from Google Fonts.
  - link previews: a 1200 x 630 share image (assets/ui/share.png), Open
    Graph and Twitter tags, a canonical URL and a favicon made from the
    original's jet. The URLs assume https://restless-forge.dev/games/foobar/.
  - deploy package: index.html, js/, data/ and assets/ only.
  - browser test: fails on any request outside the page's own origin, and
    the high score check reads the final score instead of assuming it, since
    missiles still in the air can score during the dying frames.

CHANGED IN 24
  - lag: the side panel writes only what changed and the boss meter moves
    without layout. Frames over 33 ms in a throttled browser went from 0.8 a

  - briefings: the side panel switches to the new level's boss first.
  - house rule dronesVisibleInFreeze.
  - QA shortcuts: bbb skips to just before the boss arena, fff shows the
    frame rate.
