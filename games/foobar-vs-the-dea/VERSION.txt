CHANGED IN 26
  - touch controls: a stick for the left thumb and FIRE and BOMB for the
    right, beside the playfield on a phone on its side and under it upright,
    never on it. TOUCH CONTROLS on the title and pause screens turns them on
    and off; they start on where the main pointer is a finger, and the choice
    is kept in localStorage (foobar_touch). The stick centres where the thumb
    lands and reads eight directions, so the ship still moves 4 px a frame;
    a thumb on the + between the buttons holds both. Replaces the invisible
    touch zones over the playfield, which Ken found clashed.
  - leaving the page (another tab or app, the screen locked) pauses a game in
    play and holds every sound, the intro's music too. On a phone the music
    used to play on in the background.
  - a key held through a boss kill no longer clears the next briefing: the
    page ignores the browser's auto-repeat, as the decision to wait for a
    fresh key press always meant. A held P or M no longer flickers either.
  - an iOS audio context left "interrupted" by a call or another app now
    resumes on the next tap, not only a "suspended" one.
