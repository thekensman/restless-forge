About This Forge

Restless Forge is a one-person workshop. I'm Ken. I build tools because I can't stop building things, and I share them because useful things shouldn't be locked behind sign-up walls.

Why I Build

I started building tools to solve my own problems. The first was a calculator to figure out what my time was actually worth — not my salary, but my real hourly wage after commute, taxes, and the hours I spent recovering from work. That became What Is My Time Worth?

Then I wanted to make hologram GIFs for a pyramid projector and couldn't find a decent tool that didn't require a subscription. So I built HoloPath — a 13-stage rendering pipeline that turns any image or video into animated holographic art, entirely in the browser.

SandPath came from watching kinetic sand tables trace patterns and wanting to feed them my own art. It converts images and SVGs into .thr and .gcode files for Oasis, Sisyphus, and ZenXY tables.

Philosophy

Every tool on Restless Forge follows a few principles:

  • Free and open source. The code is on GitHub. Always.
  • Privacy-first. Calculations happen in your browser. No data is sent to servers. No cookies, no tracking.
  • No gatekeeping. No accounts, no paywalls, no "sign up to unlock." If you need the tool, use it.
  • Honest and useful. I'd rather build something small that works well than something big that doesn't.

The Tools

  • What Is My Time Worth? — Real hourly wage calculator, DIY-vs-hire decision engine, and job comparison tool.
  • HoloPath — Hologram GIF generator with 5 presets, 13 effect stages, and 5 output layouts.
  • SandPath — Image and SVG to sand table pattern converter (.thr / .gcode).
