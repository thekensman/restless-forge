Sites I Like

A small, curated list of sites from friends, family, and people whose work I enjoy. These are personal recommendations — not affiliated with Restless Forge, and nothing here is sponsored.

The internet used to have more of this. Before search became the only way anyone found anything, sites linked to other sites because a person had actually been there and thought it was worth your time. That habit mostly died, and what replaced it — link exchanges, paid placements, "resources" pages assembled to game a ranking — is worse in every way that matters.

What earns a place here

Only one thing: I know the person, or I use the site. Usually both. Every entry is somewhere I would send a friend without qualifying it, which is a higher bar than it sounds and the reason this list is short.

Nothing on this page is paid for. There are no affiliate links, no tracking parameters, no arrangement where a link here buys a link somewhere else. If that ever changes, it will be labelled on the page rather than mentioned in a policy nobody reads.

Entries do get removed. A site that goes dark, changes hands, or turns into something I would not recommend comes off the list — this is reviewed periodically rather than left to rot, which is the usual fate of pages like this one.
