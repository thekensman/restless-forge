Restless Forge

A workshop for making things that matter

Free, open-source tools for people who want to understand their time, create holographic art, and convert images into sand table patterns. No sign-ups. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.

Financial

What Is My Time Worth?

Calculate your real hourly wage after commute, taxes, and hidden costs. Then decide when to DIY and when to hire. Based on the Your Money or Your Life methodology.

Launch Calculator
Creative

HoloPath

Transform any image, GIF, or video into animated hologram art with scan lines, glow, glitch effects, and more. Supports pyramid projectors, LED fans, and Pepper's Ghost displays.

Launch Generator
Utility

SandPath

Convert images and SVG files into sand table patterns (.thr and .gcode) for kinetic sand tables like Oasis, Sisyphus, and ZenXY. Automatic image tracing with live preview.
