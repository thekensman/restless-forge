Privacy at Restless Forge

Last updated: March 2026

The Short Version

Almost all tools on Restless Forge are frontend-only. Your calculations, images, and files are processed entirely in your browser and nothing is sent to our servers. A small number of tools use server-side processing and are clearly labeled with a "☁ Cloud-assisted" badge — see the Cloud-Assisted Tools section below for exactly what they send. Restless Forge's own code sets no cookies, and we never share or sell your data. The one exception to a cookie-free experience is advertising: Google AdSense may set cookies to serve ads — see the Advertising section below.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal data. Restless Forge does not require accounts, logins, or any form of registration. We don't use contact forms that store submissions. We don't set first-party cookies.

How the Tools Work

Each tool on this site processes data entirely in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API:

  • What Is My Time Worth? — All salary, tax, and cost calculations happen in your browser. No data is transmitted.
  • HoloPath — Images and videos are processed using Canvas 2D directly in the browser. Files are never uploaded.
  • SandPath — SVG files and image tracing are processed entirely client-side. Files are never uploaded.

Cloud-Assisted Tools

Most Restless Forge tools process everything in your browser. A small number of tools use server-side processing for features that require external services. These tools are clearly labeled with a "☁ Cloud-assisted" badge in the tool directory, and each one has its own privacy page describing its exact data flow.

Rise & Rhyme sends your Google Calendar public iCal URL to our server once per day (in the evening before your alarm). Our server fetches the calendar feed, extracts event titles and times for the next day, and sends them to the Anthropic Claude API to generate song lyrics. Event details are not stored after the lyrics are generated. The generated lyrics are returned to your browser and cached there. Your iCal URL and your IP address are stored only as SHA-256 hashes for rate-limiting purposes — neither is retained in raw form — and both are deleted automatically once their rate-limit window closes. Full details: Rise & Rhyme privacy.

Advertising

Restless Forge uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics service. Basic server logs (IP address, URL, timestamp) are retained for up to 14 days for security and debugging purposes, then deleted.

Third-Party Links

This site contains links to external services:

  • Substack — If you subscribe, you share your email with Substack under their privacy policy.
  • Ko-fi and Buy Me a Coffee — Donations are processed by those platforms under their own policies.
  • GitHub — Source code is publicly available.
